Stay Here privacy policy

Español

Updated October 26, 2026. This policy applies to Stay Here 1.1.4.

Stay Here helps block unwanted popup windows and tabs. Protection is processed in your browser. The extension has no servers of its own, accounts, analytics or advertising, and does not transmit browsing data to the developer.

Data used by the extension

The extension uses page addresses, link or form destinations, site domains and navigation events to decide which openings to allow or close. It does not extract the contents of form fields or create a permanent browsing history.

Preferences, including the panel language and allowed domains, are saved in chrome.storage.local within your Chrome profile. They remain until you change them or uninstall the extension.

Tab addresses, temporary navigation authorizations, counters, the last blocked domain and protected-document identifiers are kept in memory and chrome.storage.session. Tab state is deleted when a tab closes; session storage is cleared when the browser session ends. Chrome also clears it when the extension is disabled, reloaded or updated.

Permissions

  • Access to HTTP and HTTPS sites: detect links and apply protection to supported pages and frames.
  • storage: save preferences and temporary protection state.
  • webNavigation: identify openings and navigations and perform the optional return after certain redirects.
  • scripting: install document protection using options supplied by the extension, without accepting configuration changes from the website.

Sharing and optional support

The extension does not sell or share browsing data with third parties. It does not load external scripts, images or widgets to receive support payments. The Ko-fi symbol is bundled locally.

The Ko-fi page opens only when you click the support button. Payments take place on that external site and are subject to Ko-fi's and its payment processor's policies. Stay Here does not receive your card details. Every extension feature remains free.

Control and contact

You can allow a site or pause protection from the panel. Reload open pages to apply all changes. Uninstalling the extension deletes its saved data; reload open pages afterwards to remove protection already installed in them.

For questions, use Stay Here Discussions. For vulnerability reporting, follow the repository's security policy.
